# Google sign-in branding asset

`android/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/google_g.png` is the standard Google G icon supplied by Google's identity documentation:

- [Source asset](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png)
- [Sign in with Google branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines)

The icon is used to identify the Sign in with Google action. Google brand features remain Google's property and are used under the linked branding guidelines.

The Google Sans font used only for that button is from the [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/googlesans). Its [OFL license](GoogleSans-OFL.txt) and [trademark notice](GoogleSans-TRADEMARKS.md) are included alongside this file.
